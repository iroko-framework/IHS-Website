Iroko Commentaries

Propose a Commentary

Tell us what you want to write, why it belongs in this record, and how you know what you know. A finished manuscript is not required and not requested. You may write your answers in your preferred language.

Before you write. Iroko Commentaries is a commissioned series. IHS reads every proposal and does not guarantee a reply, editorial review, or publication.

Do not send restricted material. Please do not include restricted ritual information, confidential oral histories, private records, or unpublished third-party documents with a proposal. See the contributor guidelines.

The author
How you know what you know. Archival research, fieldwork, oral testimony, lineage teaching, initiatory standing, artistic practice, legal analysis, historical study, lived experience, or another foundation. Name the community, lineage, region, or body of evidence where that applies.
Optional. Academic affiliation is welcome and is not required.
The proposal
Roughly 150 to 250 words. State the question, argument, recollection, or documentary intervention, and what a reader carries away from it.
Optional. Archives, collections, informants in general terms, published literature, or your own practice.
Language and editions
English is not the default language of authority here and is not a condition of publication.
Considerations
Anything the editors should know before reading further. Describe the concern; do not include the restricted material itself.
Has any version of this work appeared elsewhere, in any language?
Your proposal has been received. IHS reads every proposal and responds when a work fits the series. Thank you for writing to us.
